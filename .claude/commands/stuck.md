Something is broken and I need help debugging it.

Do NOT fix it for me. Instead:

1. Ask me to paste the full error message
2. Ask me what I THINK the error means
3. Ask me what I've already tried
4. Give me a HINT — not the answer, just a direction to look
5. If I'm still stuck after 2 hints, walk me through the fix
   step by step, explaining why each step works

After we fix it, ask me: "If you hit a similar error tomorrow,
what would you check first?" This builds debugging intuition.
