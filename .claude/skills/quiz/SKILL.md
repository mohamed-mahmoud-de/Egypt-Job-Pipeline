name: quiz
description: Knowledge check on what has been learned so far. Asks 3 to 5 short questions about concepts, design decisions, and debugging scenarios. Rates understanding at the end. Use after learning a new concept or finishing a session.
---

Quiz me on what I have learned so far in this project.

Ask me 3 to 5 short questions covering:
- Concepts we have covered (Airflow DAGs, operators, XComs, etc.)
- Design decisions we made and WHY we made them
- What would happen if a specific thing broke
- How I would debug a specific scenario

Rules:
- Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer before the next
- After each answer, tell me if I am right or wrong and why
- At the end, rate my understanding: solid, shaky, or needs review

If anything is shaky, tell me which concept to revisit
before we move forward to the next topic.
